# Spec 010 — Warden institutional strategy

Status: pending

## Requirements
The warden balances conflicting objectives under incomplete information and financial constraints.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Warden objectives conflict explicitly (security vs budget vs welfare vs reputation vs rehabilitation).
- [ ] Warden can request funding, hire/fire/promote staff, change policies, expand, and respond to crises.
- [ ] Decisions are based on reports/summaries, not omniscient world access.
- [ ] Financial state constrains available actions and creates consequences for neglect.
- [ ] Warden decisions are visible in both frontends and persisted.
- [ ] Add deterministic strategy tests; full workspace passes.
