# Spec 008 — Gang politics and social structure

Status: pending

## Requirements
Gangs must be dynamic organizations with internal disagreement, not monolithic blocks.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Gangs can form, recruit, lose members, fracture, change leadership, and form/rival alliances based on relationships and needs.
- [ ] Internal member motivations can disagree with leadership and produce detectable disputes.
- [ ] Gang history records leadership changes, mergers/splits, and major conflicts.
- [ ] Gang changes influence security, contraband, and incident generation.
- [ ] Expose meaningful gang detail in both frontends.
- [ ] Add deterministic tests; full workspace passes.
